# CRB (Cross River Bank)

> CRB (Cross River Bank) is a New Jersey-chartered, FDIC-insured bank and financial technology infrastructure provider founded in 2008 and headquartered in Fort Lee, New Jersey. Its API-driven core, the Cross River Operating System (COS), lets fintech companies, neobanks, lenders, and platforms embed banking, payments, cards, and lending. Access uses OAuth2 client credentials with an isolated sandbox, Postman collection, webhooks, and a dated changelog.

## APIs

- [Cross River Operating System (COS) API](https://docs.crossriver.com): Accounts and Payments products spanning ACH, RTP, FedNow, wires, international payments, cards, subledgers, and lending.
